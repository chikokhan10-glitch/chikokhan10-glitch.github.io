ANIMAL HEALTH

🐄 Mastitis kwa Ng'ombe

Mastitis ni inflammation ya mammary gland ya mnyama anayetoa maziwa. Ni tatizo muhimu katika dairy farming kwa sababu linaweza kupunguza uzalishaji na kubadilisha ubora wa maziwa.

Sababu

  • Maambukizi ya microorganisms.
  • Usafi duni wakati wa kukamua.
  • Majeraha kwenye teat au udder.
  • Mazingira machafu na wet bedding.

Dalili

  • Udder kuwa na joto.
  • Uvimbe na maumivu.
  • Mabadiliko ya appearance ya maziwa.
  • Milk yield kupungua.
  • Mnyama anaweza kuwa na fever katika severe cases.

Diagnosis

Clinical examination inaweza kusaidia kugundua mastitis. Tests za milk kama California Mastitis Test (CMT) zinaweza kusaidia kutambua inflammation. Culture inaweza kufanyika inapohitajika.

Prevention

  • Usafi wa mikono kabla ya milking.
  • Safisha teat na vifaa vya kukamulia.
  • Weka mazingira ya ng'ombe katika hali safi.
  • Chunguza udder mara kwa mara.
  • Tenga na manage animals wenye infection kulingana na ushauri wa mtaalamu.
⚠️ Muhimu: Usitumie antibiotic bila diagnosis na ushauri wa mtaalamu. Zingatia dose, withdrawal period na sheria za matumizi ya veterinary medicines.
ANIMAL HEALTH

🐕 Rabies – Kichaa cha Mbwa

Rabies ni ugonjwa wa viral unaoathiri central nervous system ya mammals na unaweza kuathiri binadamu.

Transmission

Mara nyingi virus huenea kupitia saliva ya mnyama aliyeambukizwa, hasa baada ya bite au exposure ya saliva kwenye jeraha.

Dalili zinazoweza kuonekana

  • Mabadiliko ya tabia.
  • Aggression au unusual quietness.
  • Excessive salivation.
  • Difficulty swallowing.
  • Neurological signs.

Prevention

  • Vaccination ya mbwa na paka.
  • Responsible ownership.
  • Kudhibiti roaming animals.
  • Kuepuka kushika mnyama anayeshukiwa bila PPE.
⚠️ Mtu akiumwa: Safisha jeraha mara moja kwa maji mengi na sabuni, kisha tafuta huduma ya afya haraka kwa ajili ya assessment ya post-exposure treatment.
ANIMAL HEALTH

🐔 Newcastle Disease

Newcastle disease ni ugonjwa wa viral unaoweza kuathiri kuku na ndege wengine. Katika outbreaks baadhi ya flocks zinaweza kupata mortality kubwa.

Dalili

  • Coughing na sneezing.
  • Nasal discharge.
  • Greenish diarrhea.
  • Depression.
  • Drop in egg production.
  • Neurological signs.

Uenezaji

Ugonjwa unaweza kuenea kupitia birds, equipment, clothing, footwear, visitors na contaminated materials.

Biosecurity

  • Punguza unnecessary movement.
  • Sanitize equipment.
  • Tenga sick birds.
  • Quarantine new birds.
  • Dhibiti visitors.
  • Fuata vaccination program inayofaa.
💡 Kumbuka: Dalili zinaweza kufanana na magonjwa mengine. Diagnosis ya kitaalamu ni muhimu kabla ya kufanya maamuzi ya disease control.
FARMING

🌱 Nutrition ya Mifugo

Lishe bora ni msingi wa growth, production, reproduction na immunity ya mifugo.

1. Maji

Mifugo inahitaji maji safi na salama. Water requirement hubadilika kulingana na species, production, temperature na aina ya feed.

2. Roughage

Pasture, hay na silage ni sources muhimu za fiber hasa kwa ruminants.

3. Concentrates

Concentrates zinaweza kutoa energy na protein pale ambapo forage pekee haitoshi kukidhi mahitaji ya mnyama.

4. Minerals na Vitamins

Minerals na vitamins zinahitajika kwa mfumo wa mifupa, reproduction, immunity na metabolic functions.

Tips za lishe bora

  • Toa maji safi.
  • Tumia feed yenye ubora.
  • Epuka moldy feed.
  • Badilisha ration taratibu.
  • Balance energy, protein, fiber na minerals.
